Privacy Policy

1. Single Purpose

ZeroWeb AI is designed exclusively to detect and block standalone AI platforms, search engine AI overviews, and embedded chatbot widgets in strict accordance with the Google Chrome Web Store Single Purpose Policy.

2. Zero-Data Collection Guarantee

ZeroWeb AI does NOT collect, transmit, log, or sell any user data whatsoever:

  • Zero personally identifiable information collected.
  • Zero browsing history or search queries tracked.
  • Zero keystrokes or webpage contents intercepted.
  • Zero analytics SDKs or remote connections.

3. Local Device Storage

We use chrome.storage.local strictly on your device to persist:

  • Your master shield toggle state.
  • Your user-whitelisted domains.
  • Anonymous numeric count of blocked elements.

This data never leaves your browser and is erased upon uninstalling.

4. Chrome Permissions

  • declarativeNetRequest: Native network blocking of known AI domains.
  • storage: Storing your settings and whitelist locally.
  • tabs: Reading active tab domain to allow 1-click whitelisting.
  • Host permissions (<all_urls>): Suppressing AI overviews in the DOM.
