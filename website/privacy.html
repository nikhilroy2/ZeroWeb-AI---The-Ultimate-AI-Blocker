Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Single Purpose Statement

ZeroWeb AI ("the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") is designed exclusively to detect, block, and hide standalone generative AI platforms, search engine AI overviews, social media AI triggers, and embedded chat assistants. We strictly adhere to the Google Chrome Web Store Single Purpose Policy.

2. Zero-Data Collection Guarantee

ZeroWeb AI does NOT collect, transmit, log, monetize, or sell any personal information or web browsing data whatsoever.

  • We do NOT collect personally identifiable information (names, emails, credentials).
  • We do NOT track or record your browsing history or search queries.
  • We do NOT intercept keystrokes, form submissions, or webpage contents.
  • We do NOT embed analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or tracking pixels.
  • We do NOT transmit data to any external server or cloud endpoint.

3. Local Storage Usage

ZeroWeb AI uses the standard chrome.storage.local API purely to persist your user-configured preferences locally on your physical device. These items include:

  • Your master protection toggle state (On/Off).
  • Your selected protection categories (Standalone AI, Search Overviews, Social Media AI, Chatbots).
  • Your user-defined whitelisted domains and custom blocked domains.
  • Your custom CSS selectors.
  • Aggregated, anonymous numeric counters of blocked items (e.g., total items blocked) displayed in your toolbar popup.

This data never leaves your computer and is immediately erased if you choose to uninstall the extension or click "Reset to Defaults".

4. Browser Permissions Justification

In full accordance with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, ZeroWeb AI requests only the minimum necessary permissions required to fulfill its core function:

  • declarativeNetRequest: Required to block and redirect network traffic to known standalone AI domains (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) with native browser efficiency.
  • storage: Required to store your configuration, custom rules, and local statistics locally on your machine.
  • tabs: Required exclusively to determine the hostname of the currently active tab so the toolbar popup can display site-specific whitelist controls and page statistics.
  • alarms: Required to automatically expire temporary 10-minute bypasses without consuming background CPU cycles.
  • Host Permissions (<all_urls>): Required to inject the content blocker script across search engines, social platforms, and websites to hide embedded AI overviews and chatbot overlays.

5. Chrome Web Store Compliance & Limited Use

ZeroWeb AI strictly complies with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements:

  • The Extension's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • We do not allow any third-party access to local data.
  • We do not use local data for personalized advertising, credit assessment, or lending purposes.

6. Changes to this Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted here along with a revised effective date. Because we collect zero contact information, we recommend reviewing this page periodically.
