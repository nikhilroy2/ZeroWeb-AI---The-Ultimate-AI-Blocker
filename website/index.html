Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Does ZeroWeb AI slow down my browsing?

Quite the opposite! By utilizing Chrome's native DeclarativeNetRequest API and document_start CSS injection, ZeroWeb AI prevents bulky AI scripts, WebSockets, and heavy widgets from downloading, speeding up page load times and saving bandwidth.

Can I temporarily unblock a specific site?

Yes. Clicking the extension icon in your browser toolbar lets you whitelist the active website with one click or grant a 10-minute temporary bypass with automatic re-blocking.

Does ZeroWeb AI collect any of my browsing data?

No. ZeroWeb AI has a strict Zero-Data policy. The extension contains zero tracking libraries, zero telemetry, and zero remote server connections. All operations happen strictly on your local computer.

Can I block custom AI domains or CSS selectors?

Yes! In the extension Options dashboard, you can add custom domain wildcards and custom CSS selectors to hide any unwanted element across the web.
